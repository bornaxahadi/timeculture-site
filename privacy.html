Privacy Policy

Time Culture · Last updated September 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Time Culture ("we," "our," "us") collects, uses, and protects information in connection with our website, social media accounts, and our integration with TikTok's API.

1. Information we collect

When you interact with our content (for example, by commenting, messaging, or following our accounts), the platform you are using shares limited information with us, such as your username, public profile information, and the content of your comments or messages, in accordance with that platform's own privacy policy.

2. TikTok data

Our TikTok integration is used to manage and publish content to our own connected TikTok account(s), and to read public engagement data (views, likes, comments) on our own content. We do not collect, store, or process personal data belonging to other TikTok users beyond what is necessary to respond to public comments on our own posts.

3. How we use information

  • To respond to comments and messages sent to our accounts
  • To understand which content performs well, so we can improve future posts
  • To operate our own social media accounts, including scheduling and publishing content

4. Data sharing

We do not sell personal information. We do not share information with third parties except as required to operate our accounts or as required by law.

5. Data retention

We retain information only for as long as necessary for the purposes described above, or as required by the platforms we use.

6. Your rights

You may contact us at any time to ask what information we hold about you, or to request that we stop processing it, subject to the platform's own capabilities and policies.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "last updated" date above reflects the most recent version.

8. Contact

For questions, reach out via direct message on Instagram @timecultur.
